More room to work. A more capable browser. Now on Intel Macs, too.

This update brings your everyday tools closer, makes it easier to work beside your agents, and smooths out the small interruptions that get in the way.

## Intel Macs join the workspace

Molly now supports **Intel and Apple Silicon Macs**, with a build for each. Both require macOS 13 or later.

## Your tools, within reach

### Browser, Terminal, Files, and Source control have their own sidebar shortcuts

Open the tool you need directly, without going through the Workspace dropdown.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/sidebar-tools.png" alt="Molly’s sidebar shortcuts beside the project list." width="465">

### Keep your tools beside your conversation

Browse, use a terminal, or inspect files while your chat stays in view. Expand a tool to full width when you need more space, then return to your previous layout.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/multitasking.png" alt="A live browser page beside a draft in the Fieldnotes project." width="1000">

### Arrange the sidebar your way

Drag its shortcuts into your preferred order. Molly remembers the arrangement, including tools that are temporarily unavailable. You can also choose which side of the window the shortcut rail sits on.

### Know what unavailable tools are for

File preview, Source control, Side chat, and Pull request details remain discoverable, with hints explaining when they can be used.

### Search without covering your work

Search now lives in the sidebar, with clearer filters for chats, projects, actions, and settings. Your query and filters stay in place when you switch away and return.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/search.png" alt="Sidebar search with category filters and a matching project." width="465">

### Collapse the sidebar consistently

Closing it also hides the selected tool, while preserving its state for when you reopen it.


## A browser built for everyday use

### Tabs and navigation

### A dedicated browser tab bar

Create and switch between pages, drag tabs into a new order, and keep the active page in view when the strip gets crowded. Tabs can also be reordered with the keyboard.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/browser-tabs.png" alt="Several real websites open in Molly’s browser tabs." width="1000">

### More useful tab menus

Rename, duplicate, reload, or copy a tab’s link. Close other tabs, tabs to either side, or all tabs from the same menu.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/browser-tab-menu.png" alt="A browser tab’s rename, reload, duplicate, copy, and close actions." width="620">

### Preview a tab before switching

Pause over a tab to see its title, address, and page preview. Briefly passing over it won’t immediately open a preview.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/tab-preview.png" alt="A delayed preview showing the title, address, and thumbnail of another tab." width="430">

### Pick up where you left off

Molly saves browser tabs, their order, and the selected tab, along with back and forward history for restored pages.

### A more helpful address bar

Search the web, revisit a page, or choose from online suggestions as you type. New blank tabs put the cursor in the address bar, ready to go.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/address-suggestions.png" alt="Address-bar results combining history and Google search suggestions." width="690">

### Find text on the current page

Use Find in page from the browser menu or the usual keyboard shortcut, move between matches, and dismiss it with Escape.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/find-in-page.png" alt="Find in page with a query and match navigation controls." width="688">

### Familiar keyboard controls

Focus the address bar, create and close tabs, switch tabs, and reload while working inside a page. On Mac, Command–W closes the active browser tab before the app window.

### A clearer starting page

Return to recent pages or open a local project preview from one place.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/new-tab.png" alt="Molly’s new-tab view with recent pages and an address field." width="1000">


Online suggestions send eligible address-bar text to Google.

### History, extensions, and page tools

### History gets its own page

Open it from the three-dot menu, search visited pages, remove individual entries, or clear your saved history.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/history.png" alt="The History page with search, visited pages, and removal controls." width="1000">

### Install supported extensions directly from the Chrome Web Store

Use **Add to Molly** on an extension’s store page and review its permissions before installation. Installing from a store link remains available, too.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/web-store.png" alt="The Chrome Web Store inside Molly showing Molly’s installation integration." width="1000">

### A dedicated Extensions page

Search installed extensions, read their details and permissions, open them, or remove them. Developer controls stay out of the main view unless you need them.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/extensions.png" alt="Molly’s Extensions page with Dark Reader installed." width="1000">

### Choose what belongs on your toolbar

The extensions button opens a compact list of installed extensions. Pin up to three for quick access; new installations no longer add themselves to the toolbar automatically.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/extension-panel.png" alt="The compact Extensions panel with an explicit pin control." width="438">

### Clearer extension feedback

Extensions without a clickable action are identified, and some unsupported extensions are stopped before installation instead of leaving you with a button that does nothing.

### Save and copy screenshots as separate actions

Save screenshot asks where to put the image. Copy screenshot puts the image on your clipboard.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/browser-menu.png" alt="Separate Save screenshot and Copy screenshot actions in the browser menu." width="358">

### Inspect the current site

The address-bar icon opens a panel with the site address, HTTP or HTTPS information, and available cookie count.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/site-info.png" alt="Site information showing the address scheme and available cookies." width="630">

### Easier page annotations

The send button is visible again, and hovering over the Molly icon reveals a cancel control.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/annotation.png" alt="The page annotation composer with its visible send arrow." width="750">


Extension compatibility varies. Some Chrome features remain unavailable, and a page that was already open may need one reload after installing an extension. Extension settings stored through the supported storage APIs stay in your Molly profile; they do not sync through a Google account.

## Smoother work in larger projects

### Less waiting during startup

Molly reconnects more promptly while its local services finish starting and reuses work from earlier launches.

### Lighter workspace refreshes

Git changes and file updates do less repeated work, especially when you have several projects open.

### More responsive change review

Large diffs are prepared away from the main interface, and long file lists render as you scroll.

### Large pull requests load file by file

Open the changes you want to inspect without loading every closed file. Very large individual diffs can be read in chunks, with retry controls when a file fails to load.

### A clearer Source control view

Filenames, folders, staged changes, and unstaged changes are easier to scan. One failed file preview no longer has to block the rest of the list.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/source-control.png" alt="A changed file with its folder, addition count, and staging controls." width="680">

### Less memory overhead during fragmented agent responses

Molly handles small incoming pieces more efficiently while keeping its existing response limits.


## The small things, made better

### More consistent controls

Improved spacing, icon alignment, button sizes, corner shapes, and text hierarchy across browser controls, search results, tool tabs, and menus.

### Menus over live pages

Addressed cases where opening menus or hovering over controls hid or interrupted the browser page beneath them.

### Steadier menus

Improved menu borders, placement, and dismissal, including the profile menu and tab context menus. Right-click menus no longer begin with an unintended item highlighted.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/profile-menu.png" alt="Molly’s profile menu with its stable outline and settings entry." width="340">

### Readable address suggestions

Improved address-suggestion hovering and selection, with balanced padding and an opaque background that stays readable when app glass is on.

### Quieter mouse focus

Reduced unwanted focus outlines after mouse clicks while keeping visible focus for keyboard navigation.

### Consistent glass controls

More consistent glass backgrounds across tool views. Terminal stays opaque when app glass is off, and only offers its glass controls when app glass is enabled.

### A context menu that toggles

Fixed the composer’s context button so it can close the menu it opened, without disturbing your draft.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/composer-context.png" alt="The composer context menu opened beside the draft." width="720">

### Better recovery from save errors

Reduced repeated image-error notifications and improved recovery when saving local preferences or images fails.

### More accurate browser input

Improved browser pointer accuracy when zooming or using the device toolbar.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChloeVPin/Molly-releases/main/release-notes/0.1.1/images/device-toolbar.png" alt="Molly’s browser device toolbar above a live page." width="1000">

### A cleaner production build

Hid the Intel-build development warning in production builds.
